# All-Star research icon evidence

These Institute screenshots were supplied on 2026-07-18 as the authoritative in-game tree art
from S0 through X8. The UI crops retain the badge, season marker, and completion state while
excluding labels and neighboring tiles. They are downsampled into the embedded WebP sprite in
`css/all-star-boh.css` so all 29 trees and the three T10 troop portraits add no deploy-time files.

The first screenshot supplies the fifteen base trees from Rapid Production through Accessory
Production. The second supplies Master Warfare, Master City Defence, Defence Legion Research,
Advanced Soldier Training, Imperial Guards, Guard Rally, both S3 Art of War trees, Lofty Warrior,
Raider Legion, Art of War - Command, and Solid Tactics.

The Dragon Blood Blessing capture still displays its former X8 badge. It is intentionally mapped
to X2 because the game moved that tree to X2. Lofty Legion is mapped to X8.

## Current use and preservation

The sprite consumer described above belongs to the retired BoH interface. These screenshots and their hashes remain source evidence; retaining them does not re-enable that UI. Before reusing a crop, identify the current consumer and keep the season-remapping rationale with it. Verify hashes before modifying or moving originals.
