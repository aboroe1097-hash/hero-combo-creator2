Boot Mini-Games

Pick a game, then switch anytime without leaving Arcade.

Mode A Merge Rush

Tap two matching type+color to merge up the rarity ladder. Gold pair = jackpot.

Mode B Sort the Hoard

Pieces fall from the gate. Flick/tap left (blue) or right (gold) bins before they hit the floor.

Mode C Crystal Relay

One piece on a bar. Tap when the spark is in the green zone — chain alternating sides for combos.

Mode D Set Assembly

Complete a mini set (e.g. all three feathers). Tap only matching types; junk distracts you.

Mode E Hero Rumble

Real hero icons fight! Catch blue weapons to attack, dodge gold ones. Purple = SUPER!
