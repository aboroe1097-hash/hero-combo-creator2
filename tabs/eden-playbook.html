☠️ POISON DAMAGE & HERO STRATEGY

Heavy early losses can still unlock faster growth

You can often take tiles at 97% poison damage. It will kill many troops, but that trade can be worthwhile early on. Below 50%, tile-taking becomes much easier.

Best heroes for poison tiles

  1. 1ImmortalA monster in early Eden seasons
  2. 2Rozen BladeRecommended; not pictured in the example
  3. 3Beast QueenA top poison-tile pick for many seasons

⚠️ Don’t fill your legions. Smaller troop counts reduce healing costs and keep your heroes active for more hits.
