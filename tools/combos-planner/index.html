How placing works

Edits js/combos-db.js on this computer. The S0–X2 order is fixed; you only place X8 lineups. In the Combo Generator a lineup only appears once its X8 hero is picked, so players still on X2 see nothing change.

  • Drag a lineup onto a gap, or press Place and then Place here.
  • ▲▼ nudges one row; Unplace sends it back to the X8 block at the end.
  • While placing, Place above # drops it at a rank without scrolling.
  • Keyboard: Enter on a lineup starts placing, ↑/↓ on a placed row nudges it, Esc cancels.
  • Gold names are the X8 heroes, $ marks a paid hero. New lineups from screenshots go in tools/combos-planner/x8-queue.json.
  • The tier and score are the ROC Academy source ranking; they only order the X8 list, so how the game scores a lineup can still change.
